## 1. Purpose and Philosophy

The LANA License is a memorial-focused license designed to ensure that data models, software, methodologies, datasets, and projects addressing childhood homelessness remain a pure tribute to those lost.

The core intent is to prevent the overshadowing of the cause by individuals, corporations, or institutional titles. This license prioritises the memory of the victims over the professional or social capital of the creators and implementers.

## 5. Conditions of Use

### 5.1 Mandatory Naming Convention

Any project, Derivative Work, or implementation of the Work must be named after a specific child who lost their life while experiencing homelessness. The Memorial Name must be the primary and most prominent identifier of the project across all Public-Facing Materials.

"Primary and most prominent" means that the Memorial Name must:

- appear in the project's official title;
- be used as the dominant identifier in all URLs, repository names, and metadata where technically feasible; and
- be displayed more prominently than any other name, brand, or identifier associated with the project.

### 5.2 Verification and Consent for Memorial Names

The Licensee must, prior to public release:

(a) verify the identity of the child through publicly available records, media reports, coronial findings, or equivalent official sources; and

(b) make reasonable efforts to obtain informed consent from the child's surviving parent, legal guardian, or next of kin for the use of the child's name.

Where consent cannot be obtained after reasonable efforts (for example, where no surviving family can be identified or contacted), the Licensee must document those efforts and may proceed provided the use is respectful, accurate, and consistent with the memorial purpose of this license.

Beneath the Surface AU may maintain a voluntary registry of approved Memorial Names. Use of a name from this registry constitutes compliance with this Section 5.2.

### 5.3 Restricted Identities

In all credit, attribution, promotional, and Public-Facing Materials relating to the Work or any Derivative Work, no individual, researcher, author, donor, or corporate identity shall be featured, acknowledged, or credited other than the child for whom the project is named.

This restriction applies to but is not limited to:

- individuals and personal names;
- researchers and academic affiliations;
- authors and contributors;
- financial donors and grant bodies;
- corporate sponsors and institutional partners; and
- social media accounts, conference speaker listings, and press contacts.

**Clarification:** This restriction applies to credit and attribution contexts only. It does not prohibit the discussion of individuals, researchers, officials, or organisations within the substantive content of the Work where such references are necessary for the informational, analytical, or educational purpose of the Work itself.

**Legal exception:** Where applicable law (including but not limited to charity disclosure requirements, financial reporting obligations, or electoral funding transparency laws) requires the identification of donors, sponsors, or responsible persons, the Licensee may comply with such legal requirements. Any such legally required disclosures must be presented in the minimum form necessary for compliance and must not be used as a basis for promotional credit.

### 5.4 Structural Exception for Organisational Identity

To facilitate technical implementation, the Licensee is permitted to include their organisational logo and business name within **site-wide headers and footers only**.

**Conditions of this exception:**

- Branding in headers and footers must be consistent with the Licensee's standard site-wide navigation and must not be enlarged, emphasised, or styled to draw disproportionate attention.
- The Memorial Name must remain visually more prominent than any organisational branding at all times.

This exception does **not** permit organisational branding within:

- the main body or substantive content of the Work;
- "About" pages or sections describing the project;
- hero sections, landing pages, or splash screens;
- promotional graphics, banners, or advertisements;
- credit lists, acknowledgment sections, or contributor pages;
- social media posts, metadata descriptions, or open graph tags;
- press releases, media kits, or conference materials; and
- any context where the branding could reasonably be interpreted as claiming credit for, or ownership of, the memorial project.

### 5.5 Neutral Attribution

Attribution to the underlying methodology must be provided solely through the use of the project title and its digital location. The Licensee shall credit the Work using the following functional reference:

> "Derived from the LANA Methodology (https://www.beneaththesurface.au/lana-methodology)"

This URL serves as a functional reference only and does not constitute attribution to, or endorsement by, any individual or organisation.

The names of the original creators and the organisation "Beneath the Surface AU" must be omitted from all Public-Facing Materials, except as required by this Section 5.5.
